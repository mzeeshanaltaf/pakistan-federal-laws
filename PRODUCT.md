# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Ordinary Pakistanis, journalists, students, and paralegals who have a question about a federal statute but don't already know which Act to open. Fully public, no login — anonymous per-browser sessions. Usage ranges from someone arriving with one specific question (via search or a direct link) to someone actively researching and browsing a category or a single law. Mobile and desktop both matter; there's no assumption of legal training.

## Product Purpose

Qanoon answers questions about Pakistan's federal statutes in plain language, grounded strictly in statute text, with citations that open the exact page of the exact source PDF. It exists because the corpus (525 PDFs, 21 categories) is otherwise an inert pile of files — answering a real question means already knowing which Act to open. Success is a user getting a correct, plain-language answer and being able to immediately verify it against the actual source page, without needing to trust the tool blindly.

## Brand Personality

Precise and quietly confident — the tone of a good reference librarian or a careful clerk: calm authority, exact language, understated. No sales voice, no urgency tactics, no persuasion. It earns trust by being checkable, not by asserting authority.

## Anti-references

- The navy-and-gold "legal" cliché — this is not a law firm's brochure.
- Nothing inherited from the sibling DocGenie project (no emerald, no shared visual identity).
- Corporate SaaS gloss: gradient hero sections, abstract blob illustrations, dashboard-as-marketing, hero-metric templates.
- Sterile government-portal starkness: cold, bureaucratic, unwelcoming official-forms aesthetic.
- No named reference site to anchor to — interpret "precise, quietly confident" directly rather than converging on a lookalike.

## Design Principles

- **Trust through transparency, not authority.** Every claim is checkable — citations are load-bearing UI (the reference panel, the `[n]` pills), never decorative garnish.
- **Show the boundary of what's known.** When the corpus doesn't cover a question, the interface says so plainly rather than hedging, padding, or guessing.
- **Plain language over legal register.** Dense statute text gets translated into something a non-lawyer can act on — not paraphrased-with-jargon.
- **The tool disappears, the answer stays.** Chrome, configuration, and scope controls stay quiet so the reading experience — the answer and its source — carries the page.
- **Public infrastructure, not a product funnel.** No growth-hack patterns, no artificial urgency, no upsell instinct. This is free civic utility.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA baseline across every surface: solid color contrast, full keyboard navigation, screen-reader support for streaming chat responses and the citation/reference panel. No additional user-specific accommodations noted beyond general public accessibility.
